📋 Dataset Specifications & Rules

To ensure high forecasting reliability and prevent data corruption, your CSV file must satisfy these conditions:

Required Columns (Case-Insensitive):

  • sale_date: Transaction date (e.g. 2024-01-15 or 15/01/2024)
  • product_name: Name or description of the sold product
  • quantity: Positive integer count of units sold (must be > 0)
  • unit_price: Positive unit sales price (must be > 0.0)

Optional Helpful Columns:

  • category: Classification category (e.g. Electronics, Audio)
  • region: Geographic territory (e.g. North, West, South)
  • product_id: Unique numeric product identifier
Automatic Revenue Calculation
Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price

The system automatically computes total revenue for each record and generates continuous time intervals.
