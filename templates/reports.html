5. Business Assumptions & Analytical Limitations

  • Assumption of Trend Continuity: The linear regression and moving average models assume that historical purchasing velocity and customer acquisition rates will continue into the forecast horizon without structural disruption.
  • Exogenous Market Factors: The models do not account for external macroeconomic shocks, aggressive competitor pricing, supply chain bottlenecks, or sudden inflationary pressures.
  • Zero-Sale Imputation: Any calendar days or months with no recorded transactions are imputed as zero sales to preserve time-series regularity.
  • Forecast Non-Guarantee: Future projections represent statistical approximations and expected values. Management should utilize these figures as decision support rather than deterministic guarantees.
