🛡️ Academic Principle: Why Time-Series Data Must Never Be Randomly Shuffled

In standard machine learning classification (e.g. image recognition), observations are independent and randomly partitioned. However, sales records possess temporal auto-correlation (sales tomorrow depend on sales today). If we randomly shuffled time-series records, future observations would leak into the training set (a critical flaw called lookahead bias or data leakage). To preserve realistic business validity, we strictly train on earlier historical timeframes and test exclusively on unseen future timeframes.
